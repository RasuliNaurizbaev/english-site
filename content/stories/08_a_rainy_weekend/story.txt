A Rainy Weekend

Jack had big plans for the weekend. He wanted to go hiking in the hills with his friends and have a picnic by the lake. But on Saturday morning, he looked out of the window and saw grey clouds and heavy rain. The weather forecast said: "Rain all weekend, with strong wind and a storm in the afternoon."

"Oh no," Jack said. He called his friends, and they cancelled the trip. Jack lay on the sofa and felt bored. What could he do at home for two whole days?

First, he turned on the TV, but there was nothing interesting on. Then he played a computer game for an hour, but it wasn't fun alone. He looked at his phone, read some news and checked social media. Everybody's photos looked sunny and happy, but they were all from last summer.

Then his little sister Mia came into the room. She was eight. "I'm bored too," she said. "Let's do something together!"

"Like what?" Jack asked.

"Let's make a film!" Mia said.

Jack laughed, but then he thought it was a good idea. They found an old camera in a drawer. Mia wrote a short story about a brave cat that lived on an island and looked for gold. Jack held the camera, and Mia was the actor. Their real cat, Snowy, was the star, but she didn't want to work. She just wanted to sleep!

They used blankets, pillows and boxes to build a ship in the living room. Mia's toys were the bad men on the island. They put on funny hats and old clothes from the wardrobe. During the storm in the afternoon, they recorded the sound of the thunder and the wind for their film.

On Sunday, they finished the film on Jack's laptop. They added music and wrote the title: "Captain Snowy and the Secret Island". It was only ten minutes long, but it took the whole day.

In the evening, their parents came home. Mia and Jack made popcorn, turned off the lights and showed them the film. Their parents laughed so much that their mother cried. "This is wonderful!" said their father. "You should put it online."

Jack uploaded the video, and by Monday, five hundred people had watched it. His friends sent him messages: "Your film is so funny! Can we be in the next one?"

Jack smiled. He didn't go hiking, but it was one of the best weekends of the year. "Sometimes," he thought, "rain is not so bad."
