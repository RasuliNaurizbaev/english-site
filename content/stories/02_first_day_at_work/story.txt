Tom's First Day at Work

Tom is twenty-three. Last month he finished university, and after many interviews he finally found a job. He is going to work as a designer at a small company in the city centre.

On Monday morning, Tom woke up at six o'clock. He was a little worried, so he couldn't eat much breakfast. He put on his new shirt and his best shoes, took his bag and left the flat early. He didn't want to be late on his first day.

The office was on the fifth floor of a modern building. At reception, a friendly woman smiled at him. "Good morning! You must be Tom. Welcome to the company!" She gave him a card with his name on it and took him upstairs in the lift.

His boss, Mrs Green, was waiting for him. She was about fifty, with short grey hair and kind eyes. "Nice to meet you, Tom," she said. "Let me introduce you to the team." Tom met his new colleagues: Lisa, a quiet and polite girl who works with computers; Mark, a funny man who always tells jokes; and Sam, an engineer from Canada.

Then Mrs Green showed him his desk. It was next to a big window with a wonderful view of the river. On the desk there was a new laptop, a notebook and a cup with the name of the company on it.

The morning was busy. Tom had to read a lot of information, check his email and learn the rules of the office. At first he felt confused, but his colleagues were very helpful. When he didn't understand something, Lisa explained it to him slowly.

At one o'clock, everyone went to a small café near the office for lunch. Tom ordered a chicken sandwich and an orange juice. Mark talked about his hobby: he plays the guitar in a band at the weekend. Sam told funny stories about his life in Canada. Tom laughed a lot and started to feel more relaxed.

In the afternoon, Mrs Green gave Tom his first project. He had to design a new website for a customer. "Take your time," she said. "You don't have to finish it today." Tom worked hard and forgot about the time. When he looked at the clock, it was already half past five.

Before he went home, Mrs Green stopped at his desk. She looked at his work and said, "That's a good start, Tom. I think you'll be very successful here."

On the bus home, Tom called his mother. "How was your first day?" she asked.

"Tiring, but great," Tom answered. "I think I'm going to enjoy this job."
