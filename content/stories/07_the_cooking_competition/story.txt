The Cooking Competition

Sophie loves cooking. When she was a child, she often helped her father in the kitchen. Now she is nineteen, and her friends say that her food is better than in any restaurant.

One day, she saw a sign at her college: "Cooking Competition! Cook a meal for three people in one hour. First prize: a weekend in Paris." Sophie decided to enter. She filled in the form and sent it the same day.

For the next two weeks, she practised every evening. She wanted to cook something simple but special: fish with lemon, fried potatoes and a fresh salad, and for dessert, a chocolate cake with strawberries. Her family tasted everything. "Too salty," said her brother one day. "Not sweet enough," said her mother the next day. Sophie changed the recipe again and again until it was perfect.

On the day of the competition, the college hall was full of people. There were ten students and three judges: a famous cook, a food journalist and the head of the college. Each student had a table with a cooker, an oven, pans, bowls and knives.

"You have one hour," said the famous cook. "Start now!"

Sophie worked quickly. She washed the vegetables, cut the potatoes and started to fry them. Then she mixed the flour, sugar, eggs and butter for the cake and put it in the oven. Everything was going well. But after forty minutes, she opened the oven and saw that the cake was burning! She felt terrible. There was no time to bake a new one.

Sophie stayed calm. She looked around her table. She had strawberries, chocolate and some biscuits. Quickly, she broke the biscuits into pieces and put them in glasses, then added the strawberries and melted chocolate on top. It looked lovely.

When the hour finished, the judges tasted every dish. They didn't say anything. They only wrote notes. Sophie's hands were shaking.

Finally, the famous cook stood up. "Third place goes to Tom. Second place goes to Anna. And the winner is... Sophie!" Everyone clapped. "Your fish was excellent," he said, "but the dessert was the best. It's a new idea, and it's very tasty. What do you call it?"

Sophie laughed. "I call it 'The Mistake'! My cake burned, so I made something different."

A week later, Sophie and her father went to Paris. They visited markets, bakeries and restaurants and tasted lots of new dishes. "Now I have so many new ideas," Sophie said. "Next year, I want to win again!"
