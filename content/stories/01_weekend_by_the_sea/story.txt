Anna's Weekend by the Sea

Anna is twenty-five years old. She lives in a small flat in a big city and works in an office. Her job is busy, and she is often tired. One Friday evening, her friend Kate called her.

"Let's go to the sea this weekend!" Kate said. "I've already booked a room in a hotel near the beach."

Anna smiled. "That's a wonderful idea!"

On Saturday morning, Anna got up early. She packed her suitcase: a T-shirt, shorts, a sweater, sunglasses and a towel. She had a quick breakfast, toast with butter and a cup of coffee, and went to the station.

At the station, there was a long queue for tickets. Anna waited for twenty minutes and bought a return ticket. Then a woman at the information desk said, "The train is late." Anna was worried, but Kate was calm. "Never mind," she said. "We can have a coffee and chat."

The journey took three hours. Through the window, Anna saw fields, forests, small villages and a river. When they arrived, the weather was warm and sunny. The hotel was clean and comfortable, and from their balcony they had a beautiful view of the sea.

In the afternoon, the friends went to the beach. They swam in the sea, lay on the sand and talked about their plans for the future. Kate wants to travel abroad next year. Anna hopes to find a new job with a better salary.

In the evening, they had dinner at a local restaurant. The waiter was very friendly and polite. Anna ordered fish with rice and a salad, and Kate chose a delicious seafood soup. For dessert, they both had ice cream.

After dinner, they walked back to the hotel. Suddenly Anna stopped. "Oh no! Where is my wallet?" She looked in her bag and in her pockets, but they were empty. Anna felt terrible. Her money, her credit card and her driving licence were all in that wallet.

Then they heard someone shout. It was the waiter! He was running after them with the wallet in his hand. "You forgot this on the table," he said. Anna was surprised and very happy. "Thank you so much! You're very kind," she said.

On Sunday, it started to rain, so the friends visited a museum and bought some souvenirs. In the evening, they took the train home.

"It was a short holiday, but I feel much better now," Anna said. "Let's do it again soon!"
