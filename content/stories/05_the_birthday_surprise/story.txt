The Birthday Surprise

Next Friday is Grandma Rose's seventieth birthday. Her grandchildren, Lucy and Ben, want to do something special for her. "Let's have a surprise party!" said Lucy. Ben liked the idea, but they had only one week to prepare everything.

First, they made a plan. Lucy wrote a list of guests: all the family, Grandma's old friends from work and her neighbours. Ben sent the invitations by email and text message. "Please don't tell Grandma," he wrote. "It's a secret!"

Then they had to choose a place. Grandma's house was too small for forty people, so they booked a room in a restaurant in the village. The owner was very kind and gave them a discount.

On Wednesday, Lucy and Ben went to the shopping centre to buy a present. It was difficult to decide. "What about a new bag?" asked Ben. "She already has three," Lucy answered. They looked at clothes, books and scarves, but nothing seemed right. Finally, in a small shop, Lucy found a beautiful photo album. "We can fill it with old family photos," she said. "Grandma will love it!" It was quite expensive, so they paid by credit card and shared the cost.

On Thursday evening, they collected old photos from all the family: Grandma as a little girl, at her wedding, and with her children at the beach. They put the photos in the album and wrote short messages under each one.

On Friday, Ben's job was to keep Grandma busy. He invited her for a walk in the park and then for a coffee. At the same time, Lucy and the rest of the family got the restaurant ready. They hung balloons on the walls and put flowers on every table. The waiters brought the food: salads, chicken, fish and a huge chocolate cake with seventy candles.

At seven o'clock, Ben's phone rang. It was Lucy. "We're ready!" she whispered. Ben said to Grandma, "Let's have dinner at the restaurant. I'll pay." Grandma agreed.

When they walked in, the room was dark. Suddenly the lights came on, and everyone shouted, "Surprise! Happy birthday!" Grandma was so shocked that she couldn't speak. Then she started to laugh and cry at the same time.

Everyone sang, and Grandma blew out the candles. When she opened her present and saw the old photos, she had tears in her eyes. "This is the best birthday of my life," she said. "Thank you, my dears." That night, she danced with all her grandchildren until midnight.
